How this derived database was produced
--------------------------------------

Upstream source: country extracts published by Geofabrik GmbH
(https://download.geofabrik.de/), which are themselves distributed under ODbL
1.0 and require derived databases to retain this licence.

Processing, in order:

  1. osmium tags-filter with the expressions committed at
     .github/build/expressions.txt in the swim.report repository, retaining
     natural=water, natural=coastline, natural=beach, leisure=beach_resort,
     leisure=park, leisure=nature_reserve and boundary=protected_area, together
     with all referenced members.
  2. osmium merge of the filtered per-country extracts, all taken from the same
     OSM replication timestamp.
  3. ogr2ogr (GDAL) conversion to FlatGeobuf using the tag-promotion
     configuration committed at .github/build/osmconf.ini and
     .github/build/osmconf-lines.ini.
  4. A region filter and a proximity clip (scripts/clip-layers.js) reducing each
     layer to features near a beach inside the discovery regions declared in
     src/regions.js.

Geometry in lakes-polygon.fgb is simplified with a topology-preserving
Douglas-Peucker pass at a 0.0001 degree (roughly 11 m) tolerance. Every other
published layer carries unmodified OSM geometry. No attribute values are
altered; attributes absent from the promotion configuration above are dropped.

These files are a convenience artefact for the swim.report beach discovery
pipeline, not an authoritative copy of OpenStreetMap. For the canonical data,
go to openstreetmap.org.


Not covered by this notice
--------------------------

Nothing in this notice applies to swim.report's own estimated beach hazard flag
output. Those estimates are NOT official flag status and must never be presented
as such.
